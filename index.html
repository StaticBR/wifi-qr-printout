Print a Wi-Fi QR code for your guests

This free Wi-Fi QR code generator turns your network name and password into a printable sign. Guests simply point their phone camera at the code and connect to your Wi-Fi – no more spelling out long passwords. Perfect for homes, holiday rentals, offices, cafés, restaurants and events.

How it works

  1. Enter your Wi-Fi network name (SSID) and password and choose the security type.
  2. Customize the headline, subtitle and an optional footer note.
  3. Click Print to get a clean A4 Wi-Fi card, ready to frame or pin on the wall.

Prefill with a link

All fields can be set with URL parameters, so you can bookmark or share a ready-made Wi-Fi sign:

https://staticbr.github.io/wifi-qr-printout/?ssid=MyHomeWiFi&password=secret123&title=Welcome!&footer=Enjoy%20your%20stay (opens in a new tab)

Available parameters: ssid, password, security (WPA, WEP or nopass), hidden, title, subtitle, footer, showssid, showpassword and showicon. Add print=1 to show only the finished sign without the settings – handy for displaying it on a tablet or screen. Yes/no options accept 1/0 or true/false. Special characters such as spaces must be URL-encoded (%20). Note that a password in a link is stored in your browser history.

Frequently asked questions

Which phones can scan a Wi-Fi QR code?

iPhones (iOS 11 and later) and Android phones (Android 10 and later) read Wi-Fi QR codes directly with the built-in camera app. Older phones can use any QR scanner app.

Is my Wi-Fi password safe?

Yes. The QR code is created entirely in your browser. Your network name and password are never uploaded or stored, and the page even works offline.

Which networks are supported?

WPA, WPA2 and WPA3 networks, older WEP networks, open networks without a password, and hidden networks.

Is this open source?

Yes. The source code is on GitHub: github.com/StaticBR/wifi-qr-printout.
